MIDTERM WRITING NOTES

三种题材，
轻松开写。

把格式记住，把关键信息填进去。用简单、清楚的英语，写好期中考试作文。

邀请函邮件会议纪要
背诵方法：先记每种题材的格式，再记粗体句式。方括号里的内容根据题目替换，不用原样背诵。

邀请函

Invitation Letter

写作要求

  • 称呼后直接说明邀请的活动。
  • 写清活动的时间、地点和目的。
  • 表达期待，并请对方回复。
  • 以结束语和姓名落款。

记忆顺序

称呼 → 邀请 → 活动详情 → 期待回复 → 落款
称呼和正文从左侧开始；结束语和署名靠右排列。

固定句式

I would like to invite you to [event].
我想邀请您参加……
It will be held at [place] on [date] at [time].
活动将在某日某时于某地举行。
We hope you can join us.
我们希望您能参加。
Please reply by [date].
请在……之前回复。

可以补充的句子

The purpose of the event is to [purpose].
活动的目的是……
It will be a good chance to [activity].
这将是一个……的好机会。
We would be happy to have you with us.
如果您能参加，我们会非常高兴。
Please let us know if you can come.
请告诉我们您是否能来。

完整模板

紫色为可背句式，粉色为需要按题目替换的内容，蓝色为格式标识。

Dear [Name], I would like to invite you to [event]. It will be held at [place] on [date] at [time]. The purpose of the event is to [purpose]. During the event, we will [activity 1]. We will also [activity 2]. It will be a good chance to [benefit]. We hope everyone can enjoy the event and learn something new. We would be very happy to have you with us. Please let us know if you can come by [reply date]. If you have any questions, please contact me at [contact]. We look forward to seeing you. Yours sincerely,
[Your name]

参考范文

模拟题目
假如你是李华，学校将于 12 月 12 日下午 2 点在学校礼堂举办英语日活动。请给 Green 女士写一封邀请函，介绍活动目的和主要内容，请她于 12 月 5 日前回复。

粉色是根据题目填写的内容，蓝色是格式标识；其他内容可作为固定表达。正文为 80—120 词，称呼、落款及格式标签是否计入，请以考试要求为准。

Dear Ms. Green, I would like to invite you to our English Day. It will be held at the school hall on December 12 at 2 p.m. The purpose of the event is to help students enjoy learning English. During the event, we will sing English songs. We will also share short stories. It will be a good chance to meet our students and learn about their school life. We hope everyone can enjoy the event and learn something new. We would be very happy to have you with us. Please let us know if you can come by December 5. If you have any questions, please contact me at lihua@example.com. We look forward to seeing you. Yours sincerely,
Li Hua

邮件

Email

写作要求

  • 按题目填写收件人；有抄送要求时填写 Cc。
  • 主题要具体，让人一眼看懂邮件内容。
  • 开头说明来意，正文写清时间、地点或安排。
  • 结尾表达感谢或期待回复，写上姓名。

记忆顺序

收件人与主题 → 称呼 → 来意 → 具体信息 → 结尾
收件人、主题、称呼和正文靠左；结束语和署名靠右排列。

固定句式

I am writing to tell you about [topic].
我写信是想告诉您有关……的事。
We will meet at [place] at [time].
我们将在某时于某地见面。
Please let me know if you have any questions.
如有问题，请告诉我。
I look forward to your reply.
期待您的回复。

可以补充的句子

Here are some details about [topic].
下面是关于……的一些具体信息。
First, we will [activity]. After that, we will [activity].
首先，我们将……之后，我们将……
Please arrive at [place] before [time].
请在……之前到达……
Thank you for your time and help.
感谢您花时间并给予帮助。

完整模板

紫色为可背句式，粉色为需要按题目替换的内容，蓝色为格式标识。

To: [收件人邮箱] Cc: [抄送邮箱；有要求时填写] Subject: [具体主题] Dear [Name], I am writing to tell you about [topic]. Here are some details about the plan. We will meet at [place] at [time] on [date]. First, we will [activity 1]. After that, we will [activity 2]. In the afternoon, we will [activity 3]. It will be a good chance to [benefit]. Please [important request]. I hope the information is helpful to you. Please let me know if you have any questions. Thank you for your time. I look forward to your reply. Best regards,
[Your name]

参考范文

模拟题目
假如你是李华，你的朋友 Amy 将于周五来校参观。请给她写一封邮件，告知集合时间、地点和当天的主要活动，并提醒她提前到达。

粉色是根据题目填写的内容，蓝色是格式标识；其他内容可作为固定表达。正文为 80—120 词，称呼、落款及格式标签是否计入，请以考试要求为准。

To: amy@example.com Subject: Our School Visit on Friday Dear Amy, I am writing to tell you about our school visit this Friday. Here are some details about the plan. We will meet at the school gate at 9 a.m. on Friday. First, we will visit the library and see the classrooms. After that, we will have lunch with some students. In the afternoon, we will take part in an English class together. It will be a good chance to meet new friends and learn about our school. Please arrive ten minutes early. I hope the information is helpful to you. Please let me know if you have any questions. Thank you for your time. I look forward to your reply. Best regards,
Li Hua

会议纪要

Minutes of a Meeting

写作要求

  • 写明标题、会议日期、时间和地点。
  • 列出出席与缺席人员；有特邀人员时也要写明。
  • 记录上次纪要是否通过，以及本次报告和决定。
  • 写明下次会议安排，并按题目要求签名。

记忆顺序

标题时间 → 人员 → 上次纪要 → 报告与决定 → 下次会议 → 签名

固定句式

The minutes of the last meeting were approved.
上次会议纪要获得通过。
[Name] reported that [information].
某人报告了……
It was agreed that [decision].
会议一致同意……
The next meeting will be held at [time] on [date] in [place].
下次会议将于某日某时在某地举行。

可以补充的句子

The meeting began at [time].
会议于……开始。
The members discussed [topic].
与会人员讨论了……
[Name] suggested that [suggestion].
某人建议……
More details will be discussed at the next meeting.
更多细节将在下次会议上讨论。
The meeting ended at [time].
会议于……结束。

纪要按事实记录，只选用题目中有依据的内容。

完整模板

紫色为可背句式，粉色为需要按题目替换的内容，蓝色为格式标识。

MINUTES OF A MEETING ON [TOPIC] Date: [date]　Time: [time]　Place: [place] Present: [names and roles] Absent: [names and roles] Present by invitation: [names, if any] 1. The meeting began at [time]. The minutes of the last meeting were approved. 2. [Name] reported that [information]. The members discussed [topic]. 3. [Name] suggested that [suggestion]. It was agreed that [decision]. 4. The next meeting will be held at [time] on [date] in [place]. The meeting ended at [time]. Chairperson: [signature]　Secretary: [signature]

参考范文

模拟题目
假如你是会议记录员，请根据以下信息写一份会议纪要：11 月 20 日下午 2 点在 201 室讨论学校集市；会议通过上次纪要，讨论活动安排，决定由李华在周五前列出游戏清单；下次会议于 11 月 27 日下午 2 点在 201 室举行。

粉色是根据题目填写的内容，蓝色是格式标识；其他内容可作为固定表达。正文为 80—120 词，称呼、落款及格式标签是否计入，请以考试要求为准。

MINUTES OF A MEETING ON THE SCHOOL FAIR Date: November 20　Time: 2 p.m.　Place: Room 201 Present: Amy Lee, Chairperson; Li Hua, Secretary; Tom Green, Member Absent: Anna White, Member 1. The meeting began at 2 p.m. The minutes of the last meeting were approved. 2. Amy Lee reported that the school fair would be held in December. The members discussed the activities and the things needed for the fair. 3. Tom Green suggested that each class should prepare a game. It was agreed that Li Hua would make a list of the games by Friday. 4. The next meeting will be held at 2 p.m. on November 27 in Room 201. The meeting ended at 3 p.m. Chairperson: Amy Lee　Secretary: Li Hua
考试小提醒

纪要写事实和决定，使用正式表达；没有提供的信息不要自行编造。若题目要求记录延期事项，可写：The matter was put off until the next meeting.
